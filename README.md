**Title**
Integrative Structural and Functional Analysis of Full-Length Hibernation Promoting Factor and its Association with Antibiotic Susceptibility in _Mycobacterium smegmatis_

**Organism:**  
*Mycobacterium smegmatis*

**Protein:**  
Full-length Hibernation Promoting Factor (HPF)

**HPF structural modelling:**  
[Alphafold, I-TASSER, Swiss Model, and Robetta was used for full-length HPF modelling]

**Antibiotics:**  
Paromomycin, Neomycin, Chloramphenicol

**Docking platform:**  
BIOVIA Discovery Studio — LibDock

**Residue-level interaction analysis:**  
UCSF Chimera 1.17.2 and BIOVIA Discovery Studio Visualizer

**Structural analysis and interpretation:**  
Structural visualization, residue-level interaction analysis, and interpretation of the predicted Ribosome-HPF–antibiotic complexes were performed using UCSF Chimera 1.17.2 and BIOVIA Discovery Studio Visualizer.
